picoReader UI Theme Concepts

Creative display modes for 160x128 pixel screen. Click any that excite you.

A

Game Boy Classic

Pea-green LCD palette (#0f380f, #306230, #8bac0f, #9bbc0f). Chunky pixel aesthetic. Word display styled like a Game Boy text box with a border. Menu looks like a Pokemon-style item list. Optional: scan line overlay effect.

B

Retro Terminal / CRT

Green-on-black (or amber-on-black) phosphor monitor aesthetic. Word appears with a subtle "type-on" feel. Blinking cursor after the word. Menu styled like a DOS file manager or BBS. Progress shown as [=====> ] ASCII bar.

C:\BOOKS\EARTHSEA>
wandering_
[======> ] 38% 200wpm
C

Vintage Typewriter

Warm cream/sepia background (#f4ecd8). Dark brown ink text. Word appears slightly off-center (like real typewriter imperfection). Subtle ink splatter dots around the word area. Menu styled like a library card catalog.

Pixel RPG / Text Adventure

Dark dungeon background. Word displayed in an RPG dialogue box with ornate pixel border. "HP bar" style progress. Menu looks like an RPG inventory screen. Optional: tiny sprite character in corner that reacts to reading speed.

Animated Elements

Fun additions that play during reading. These would update between words.

E

Walking Character

A tiny pixel character walks along the bottom of the screen (or along the progress bar). Walking speed matches WPM: strolling at 150, jogging at 300, sprinting at 400+. Different characters unlockable (wizard, knight, astronaut). The character IS the progress indicator.

F

Ambient Weather / Particles

Subtle particle effects in the margins: falling snow, rain drops, floating dust motes, fireflies at night. Changes based on time of day or reading duration. Purely decorative, stays out of the word area.

G

Breathing / Pulse Effect

The guide marks or border subtly pulse in sync with the word timing. Creates a rhythmic, meditative feel. At higher WPM the pulse quickens. Very subtle -- barely noticeable but adds life.

H

Page Turn Counter

A tiny book icon in the corner with a page count. Every ~250 words, the "page" flips with a mini animation. Gives a sense of physical progress. "pg 42" feels more tangible than "38%".

ORP / Bionic Reading Modes

Highlight the Optimal Recognition Point for faster reading.

I

ORP Color Highlight

The fixation letter (roughly 1/3 into the word) is displayed in the theme's highlight color. Rest of the word in normal text color. Classic Spritz-style. Works with any theme.

wandering
J

Bold-Fade (Bionic Style)

First ~40% of the word in bold/bright, rest fades to a dimmer color. Your eye grabs the bold part and fills in the rest. Works especially well at high WPM.

wandering
